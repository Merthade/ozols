Scooch privacy policy

Effective 8 August 2026

Scooch has no accounts and shows no ads. Your plans stay on your device and in your own iCloud backup.

Anonymous usage analytics

Scooch sends anonymous usage statistics to PostHog, hosted in the EU, so I can see which features get used and where people get stuck: events like "a room was added" or "a plan was exported", together with app-level context such as how many plans exist or which theme is active. No name, no email, no account, no advertising identifier, and never the contents of your plans. If you fill in the improvement survey, the text you type is included with your submission.

You can turn analytics off at any time in Settings, and the app respects that immediately.

Your plans

Everything you draw stays on your device. If iCloud is available on your device, Scooch also stores a backup of your plans in your personal iCloud storage so you can restore them on a new device. That backup lives in your private iCloud account, is handled by Apple's infrastructure, and is not accessible to me or to anyone else.

Support email

If you email emils@ozols.dev, I use your message and address only to reply. No mailing lists, no sharing.

Changes

If a future version of Scooch ever changes any of the above, this page and the App Store privacy label will be updated before that version ships.
